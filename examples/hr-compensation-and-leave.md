# Acme Inc. Compensation and Leave Guidelines

_Sample content for demonstrating retrieval-augmented generation. Acme Inc., its levels and all salary figures are fictional._
_Owner: People Ops. Classification: restricted, HR department only. Last updated: 2026._

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines describe how People Ops sets base salary, annual bonus and promotions, and how
leave is administered. They are used by HR business partners and by managers during compensation
reviews. Individual salary data is never shared outside People Ops and the employee's management
chain.

## 2. Leveling Framework

Every role at Acme is mapped to one of six levels. Levels describe scope and impact, not tenure.

| Level | Typical title | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate | Works on well-defined tasks with close guidance |
| L2 | Professional | Owns tasks end to end within a team |
| L3 | Senior | Owns projects and mentors others |
| L4 | Staff or Lead | Leads work across several teams |
| L5 | Principal or Manager | Sets direction for an area or manages a team |
| L6 | Director | Owns a function and its budget |

## 3. Salary Bands

Base salary bands are reviewed once a year in January. The figures below are annual base salary in
US dollars for location Zone A. New hires are normally offered between the minimum and the
midpoint of the band.

| Level | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | $62,000 | $71,000 | $80,000 |
| L2 | $78,000 | $90,000 | $102,000 |
| L3 | $98,000 | $114,000 | $130,000 |
| L4 | $125,000 | $145,000 | $165,000 |
| L5 | $155,000 | $180,000 | $205,000 |
| L6 | $190,000 | $222,500 | $255,000 |

### 3.1 Location zones

Because Acme is remote-first, pay is adjusted by location zone rather than by office. Zone A uses
100% of the band, Zone B uses 90% and Zone C uses 80%. Employees in the EU are paid in euros, using
the zone factor and the exchange rate fixed at the January review. An employee who relocates to a
different country for more than 30 days must notify People Ops in advance, as required by the
handbook, and the zone is reviewed at that time.

### 3.2 Pay above the band

Salaries above the band maximum are frozen until the band catches up. Salaries below the band
minimum are corrected at the next review, without waiting for a promotion.

## 4. Annual Bonus Policy

Employees on the standard compensation plan are eligible for an annual bonus. Sales roles on a
commission plan are not eligible for this bonus.

| Level | Target bonus (percent of base salary) |
|---|---|
| L1 and L2 | 5% |
| L3 | 8% |
| L4 | 10% |
| L5 | 15% |
| L6 | 20% |

The payout is calculated as target bonus x company factor x individual factor. The company factor
is set by the leadership team based on annual revenue and operating margin goals, between 0 and 1.2.
The individual factor ranges from 0 to 1.5 and comes from the year-end performance review.

Bonuses are paid with the March payroll. Employees must be employed on the payout date to receive a
bonus. Employees who joined during the year receive a prorated bonus based on months worked.
Employees who joined after October 1 are not eligible until the following year.

## 5. Promotion Cycle

Promotions happen twice a year, effective April 1 and October 1.

1. Managers submit nominations six weeks before the effective date, with a written case against
   the expectations of the next level.
2. A calibration committee for each function reviews all nominations together, so standards are
   consistent across teams.
3. People Ops checks that the proposed salary is at least the minimum of the new band.
4. Decisions are communicated by the manager in the week before the effective date.

An employee must normally spend at least 12 months in a level before promotion. A promotion comes
with a base salary increase of 8% to 12%, or an increase to the band minimum if that is higher.
Promotions to L5 and L6 also require approval from the department's executive.

## 6. Merit Reviews

Merit increases are decided once a year and take effect on March 1. The merit budget is set by
Finance each year and allocated by position in band: employees lower in their band receive a larger
share of the budget than employees near the maximum.

## 7. Leave Administration

The leave rules below match the company handbook. People Ops administers them in the HR system.

| Leave type | Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Paid vacation (PTO) | 20 days per year | Accrues monthly, rollover up to a maximum of 30 days |
| Company holidays | 10 days per year | Published each December for the next year |
| Sick leave | No cap | A doctor's note may be required after five consecutive days |
| Parental leave | 16 weeks, fully paid | Taken any time within the first year after birth or adoption |
| Bereavement leave | 5 days | For immediate family; up to 10 days for travel abroad |
| Jury duty | Paid for the full duration | Proof of service required |
| Unpaid leave | Up to 3 months | Requires manager and People Ops approval |
| Sabbatical | 4 weeks, fully paid | After five years of continuous service |

PTO requests for absences longer than three days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance.
Unused PTO above the rollover cap is forfeited on January 1. Where local law requires payout of
accrued vacation at the end of employment, People Ops follows the local rule.

## 8. Confidentiality

Compensation data is restricted. Managers may see salary and bonus data only for their own reports.
Requests from other departments for compensation data, including requests from Finance for
budgeting, are answered with aggregated figures prepared by People Ops.
